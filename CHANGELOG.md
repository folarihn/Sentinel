- Mission lifecycle and seven enforceable templates; pause and expiry enforcement.

Release remains a candidate pending the reviewed pin, approved license/public release and final checks. All ten live scenarios and manual native-human approval interaction are not fully verified. The configured free Nemotron model's initial HTTP 404 remains documented.
